This simulator lets you explore how a star's brightness changes during a planetary transit.

You can change the sizes of the star and planet, orbital inclination, and limb-darkening parameters. The visualization shows the orbital geometry and the resulting light curve.

The [blog post](/blog/transits/) explains the transit method and includes questions for using the simulator as an educational resource.
