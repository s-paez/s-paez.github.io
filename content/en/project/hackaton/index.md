The **Exoplanet Hackathon** was my undergraduate thesis in Natural Sciences Education at Universidad de La Sabana. It received a meritorious thesis distinction.

The proposal uses challenge-based learning to teach astronomy and develop scientific skills. Participants work on exoplanet problems, analyze data, formulate hypotheses, and communicate their results.

The thesis describes the design of the activities and their evaluation.
